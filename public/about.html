About Nawab Marketing

Our Story

A decade of honest dealing in real estate

Founded in 2013, Nawab Marketing began as a small property consultancy in Lahore with one goal: make property buying and selling transparent for ordinary families. Today we are a full-service real estate and marketing company working with developers, investors and overseas Pakistanis across the country.

Every file we sell, every plot we recommend and every project we market is verified by our in-house legal and survey team before it reaches a client.

Our Mission

To make real estate investment simple, transparent and accessible for every Pakistani family, at home and abroad.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted name in property marketing across Pakistan, known for integrity and after-sales support.
